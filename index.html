01 About

My work sits between geomechanics, geophysics, and scientific computing.

I treat submarine landslides as coupled physical systems. That means resolving earthquake loading, strain softening, terrain curvature, failure, and runout within one computational framework.

My current goal is to make these models fast enough for practical hazard assessment. I work with GPU acceleration and data-driven methods while keeping the underlying mechanics explicit and testable.

02 Selected research

Earthquake-triggered submarine landslides

A depth-averaged model for the full co-seismic evolution of a slide.

The model combines three-component seismic loading, strain-softening behaviour, and terrain curvature. GPU implementation reduced computation time by about 10 times compared with the CPU version.

Case 01

Southwest Iberian margin

Large-scale slide evolution across complex seafloor topography.

Case 02

St. Niklausen slide

Reconstruction of a documented slide in Lake Lucerne, Switzerland.

Parameter study

Failure patterns under different PGAs

Three earthquake intensities produce distinct initiation and propagation patterns.
